Questions

Questions

01Does the app need to be open?

No. Close it and lock your phone; the reminder still comes. That is why it asks for “Always” location.

02Will it drain my battery?

Not noticeably. The phone itself watches your location, and Doornote only wakes for a few seconds when you leave.

03Which distance should I pick?

100 m works best for most homes. 50 m reminds you sooner, but if your phone’s location drifts it can go off while you’re still inside.

04My Android phone never reminds me.

Some brands put apps to sleep to save battery. Doornote asks once to be left awake and shows the extra setting for phones like Xiaomi and Samsung. Settings › Diagnostics shows what is missing.

05Is Premium a subscription?

No. It is a single purchase. On a new phone signed in to the same Apple or Google account, use Restore purchases in Settings.

06Can I try it before I leave?

Yes. Settings › Send a test reminder shows the notification exactly as it will arrive.
